French School Holidays helps you automate Homey around French school vacations by zone (A/B/C, Corsica, overseas) and regional public holidays, using official government calendars. By default the app uses Zone A and Metropolitan France; choose your school zone and public holiday region in Settings, or use "Suggest from Homey location" to pre-fill both from your Homey's address (always verify — the zone depends on the school academy).

Calendars are cached on Homey (vacations up to 90 days, public holidays up to 180 days) so the app keeps working offline while the cache is valid, with a nightly recalc around 00:05 Paris time to update your Flows. Use "Force sync" in Settings to re-download calendars immediately after changing zone or region. The Homey UI is available in French and English; calendar labels stay in official French by default, with an optional English translation.
